About Dekso International LLC



 

Our Story & Mission

Founded in 2007 in California, USA, Dekso International LLC is a global commodity brokerage firm specializing in agricultural products, food ingredients, and scrap materials. With deep industry experience and a strong international network, we help customers secure the right products at the right time — reliably, efficiently, and competitively.

Built on a “client-first, integrity-driven” business model, Dekso International LLC has developed long-standing relationships across the grain, food, and scrap sectors. We invite you to explore our website and discover the wide range of commodities we can source to support your business. If you do not see an item listed, please feel free to contact us.
